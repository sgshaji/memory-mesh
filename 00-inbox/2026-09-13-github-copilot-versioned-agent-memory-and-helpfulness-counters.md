## Observations
- [behaviour] Letta MemFS documents Git-backed Markdown/YAML memory, selective loading, versioned skills, and shared memory. The ACE paper describes playbooks with strategies, failure modes, and helpful/harmful counters.
- [procedure] When positioning an experience-learning system, distinguish the complete evidence-governed reuse and evaluation workflow from these existing primitives. Evaluate incremental benefit against a comparable baseline instead of claiming memory, versioning, or feedback counters are individually novel.
- [limitation] Vendor capabilities and author-reported results on other benchmarks do not establish performance on a user's tasks or worldwide novelty. Background reflection cost and human review effort must not disappear from the comparison.
- [evidence] Public-source research checked https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-docs-md/blob/main/concepts/memfs/index.md and https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618v1 in September 2026. No live-agent benchmark was performed in this session.
