## Observations
- [procedure] Use structured intent clarification, divergent/convergent idea refinement, and adversarial review before selecting a direction; then require customer, market, technical, and economic evidence before a go, pivot, or stop decision.
- [limitation] The idea-refine rubric evaluates user value, feasibility, differentiation, and assumptions, but an LLM-guided rubric alone cannot prove demand, willingness to pay, distribution viability, or unit economics.
- [evidence] The addyosmani/agent-skills idea-refine source produces a problem statement, recommended direction, assumptions, MVP scope, and Not Doing list; its eval framework tests skill structure, routing, and agent behavior rather than whether a proposed business succeeds.
- [outcome] The skill pack is useful as a disciplined decision workflow, not as a standalone source of business-case truth.
